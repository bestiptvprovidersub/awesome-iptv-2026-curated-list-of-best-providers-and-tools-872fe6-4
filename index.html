Honest Limitations

Experience

We tested on a single ISP in one geographic region. Results may vary with different internet speeds, router configurations, or regional peering. Buffering during live events often depends on the provider’s CDN rather than overall uptime. Our tests did not account for peak-hour congestion on major sports finals day.

Method

We relied on publicly available trial subscription links. Some providers may optimise performance for trial accounts. EPG accuracy was verified manually only for 30 channels per provider – a larger sample could shift results. Tools were tested with default settings; advanced configurations might yield different outcomes.

Limitations

No IPTV service is 100% reliable. Geo-blocking, ISP throttling, and device compatibility issues are common. We strongly recommend using a VPN.
